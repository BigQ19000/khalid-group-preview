The group

The Khalid Group brings together companies in professional advice, renewable energy, and machinery for farming and construction. Each company keeps its own name, its own specialism and its own website. The group gives them a common foundation.

  • Other business services

What we stand for

Four commitments behind every company in the group

  • Quality above quantity

    We would rather do fewer things well. Every installation, product and piece of advice should hold up for years.

  • Results that matter

    We judge our work by what it changes for the client: an organisation that runs better, a home or business with reliable power.

  • Close contact

    Clients deal with people who know their situation, from the first conversation through to after-sales care.

  • A long-term view

    We build companies, client relationships and energy systems that are meant to last.
